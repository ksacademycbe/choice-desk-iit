# ChoiceDesk — Support

ChoiceDesk helps students and parents plan admission counselling with past published ranks and cutoffs. It covers:

- **Engineering, undergraduate:** JoSAA and CSAB (IITs, NITs, IIITs, GFTIs), TNEA, KEAM Engineering
- **Engineering, postgraduate:** GATE M.Tech (CCMT and COAP)
- **Medicine, undergraduate:** MCC UG (NEET), Tamil Nadu Medical UG, KEAM Medical
- **Medicine, postgraduate:** MCC PG (NEET PG)

It works offline, with no account and no ads.

## Contact

## Frequently asked questions

**Is this an official counselling app?**
No. ChoiceDesk is an independent reference app. It is not affiliated with any admission authority or any institute. Always check the rules and fill your final choices on the official counselling portal.

**Are the ranks and cutoffs a prediction?**
No. They are past published figures. Next year's cutoffs will differ.

**How do I switch to another admission?**
Tap the admission name at the top of the screen (for example "JoSAA ▾") and choose Field, then Undergraduate or Postgraduate, then the admission.

**What does a purchase unlock?**
Each admission is a separate one-time purchase (for example JoSAA Pro or MCC UG Pro). It unlocks the locked features of that admission only.

**How do I restore a purchase on a new device?**
Open the admission, then Settings (gear icon) ▸ its Pro section ▸ **Restore purchases**, signed in with the same Apple ID. If you bought ChoiceDesk Pro in version 1.0, it is restored as JoSAA Pro.

**My saved choices are missing after reinstalling.**
Turn on iCloud for ChoiceDesk in the iOS Settings app and open the app again. Data may take a few seconds to return.

**How do I change or remove my location?**
Settings (gear icon) ▸ Your location. Maps, distances and travel times are approximate, for guidance only.
