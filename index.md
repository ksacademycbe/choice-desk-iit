# ChoiceDesk IIT — Support

ChoiceDesk IIT helps JEE students and parents plan JoSAA counselling with published opening and closing ranks for IITs, IISc, NITs, IIITs and GFTIs. It works offline, with no account and no ads.

## Contact

For help, feedback or to report a data error, email ksacademy.cbe@gmail.com.
Please include your iPhone or iPad model and the app version (Settings ▸ Data, privacy & help).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is this the official JoSAA app?**
No. ChoiceDesk IIT is an independent reference app. Always fill your final choices on the official JoSAA portal.

**Are the closing ranks a prediction?**
No. They are past published figures. Next year's cutoffs will differ.

**How do I restore ChoiceDesk Pro on a new device?**
Open Settings (gear icon) ▸ ChoiceDesk Pro ▸ **Restore purchases**, signed in with the same Apple ID.

**My saved choices are missing after reinstalling.**
Turn on iCloud for ChoiceDesk IIT in the iOS Settings app and open the app again. Data may take a few seconds to return.

**How do I change or remove my location?**
Settings (gear icon) ▸ Your location.
